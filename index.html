Is everything really 100% vegan?

Yes — no dairy, whey, honey or animal-derived additives anywhere in our kitchen. Every product is certified vegan and protein content is lab-tested per batch.

Pea & rice protein isolates, tofu, tempeh, seitan, edamame, lentils and quinoa. Complete amino profiles by pairing legumes with grains.

How fresh is the food on delivery?

Cooked the same morning and delivered chilled (never frozen) in compostable packaging. Fridge life is 3 days — heat for 3 minutes and eat.
